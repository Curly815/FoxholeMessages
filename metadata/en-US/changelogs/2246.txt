What's new in 1.3.0:
- Added Trash: deleted messages/conversations are recoverable for 30 days, with manual "Delete forever" and "Empty Trash" options
- Backup and Restore now covers MMS (attachments, subject, recipients), not just SMS
- Added pinch-to-zoom for message thread text size
- Auto-compress MMS attachments now defaults to Automatic (uses your carrier's real limit)
- Moved "Support the developer" to the main Settings screen
- Renamed QK Reply to Quick Reaction Force Reply / QRF Reply
- New notification bar icon
- Removed the "Based on" row from the About screen
